## 1. Customisation → Basic info

- **Name:** `Little Choices`
- **Handle:** `@littlechoices` (fallbacks: `@littlechoicesfilms`, `@littlechoices.films`)
- **Description** (English, main):

```
Little Choices makes short animated films about the small, everyday choices that quietly shape a life — a one-minute habit, a moment of anger, an evening with the phone in a box.

Silhouette characters, no voice-over, a few honest words on screen. Every film is under five minutes and made to be watched with the people you love.

Films so far:
• 1 MINUTE — about habits
• TEMPER — about anger
• SCROLL — about phones, parents and “five more minutes”

Subscribe, and let one small choice make today a little better.
```

## 3. Settings → Channel
- **Basic info → Country of residence:** your country
- **Basic info → Keywords:**
  ```
  little choices, animated short film, short film, motivational short film, silhouette animation, life lessons, habits, self improvement, parenting, screen time, emotional short film, family
  ```
- **Advanced settings → Audience:** *No, set this channel as not made for kids* (the films are aimed at parents and adults)
- **Feature eligibility:** verify your phone number; custom thumbnails need it.

## 4. Settings → Upload defaults
**Basic info**
- Title: leave empty
- Visibility: **Private** (review every upload before publishing)
- Tags: `little choices, animated short film, short film, silhouette animation, motivational short film`
- Description (footer added to every new upload):

```

—
Little Choices — short films about the small choices that shape a life.
All films: [playlist link]
Instagram: [link] · TikTok: [link]

#LittleChoices #ShortFilm #Animation
```

**Advanced settings**
- License: Standard YouTube License
- Category: **Film & Animation**
- Video language / Title and description language: **English**
- Caption certification: *This content has never aired on television in the U.S.*
- Comments: *Hold potentially inappropriate comments for review*
- Show how many viewers like this video: on

## Known deviations
- **Music is a synthesised placeholder** (`scripts/make_audio.py`), not a real score. Drop a track at `public/audio/music.mp3` and run `npm run mix`; the loudness pass and report rerun automatically. Cue sheet: `MUSIC_CUES.md`.
- Act 3's "near silence" is about 1–2 dB under the surrounding shots (fridge hum + low swell under the drawing), not true silence, to respect the 6 dB scene-to-scene rule.
- Mix chain: premix → gain to −14 LUFS → oversampled peak limiter (−3 dBFS @ 192 kHz, catches transients) → linear two-pass loudnorm. Without the limiter the SFX transients forced loudnorm into its dynamic (compressing) mode.
- Fonts are bundled locally (`public/fonts`) instead of `@remotion/google-fonts`; same faces.
- Shots are grouped per act in `src/scenes/Act1..5.tsx` (plus the style-frame shots) rather than one file per shot.

## Vertical cut (1080×1920)
- Built from the same shots, re-framed per shot (`src/vertical.tsx`): each shot has keyframed focus/scale so the camera follows whoever speaks or acts (e.g. S1 pans from Mia to Dad on "Five minutes."); wide two-shots and book inserts use a 1.1–1.6× frame with soft black bars.
- Subtitles and the SCREEN TIME meter move into the visible part of the frame and stay out of the right 15 %; all overlay text sits above the bottom 20 % (S10's thought line is moved below the phone).
- Narration, quote and title cards are laid out natively in portrait (text within the middle 70 %, balanced wrapping).
- Same audio mix as the landscape film, so the loudness numbers above apply unchanged.

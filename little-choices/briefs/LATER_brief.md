# BRIEF — Animated short film "LATER" (Little Choices, episode 4)

Director, animator and editor brief for a ~5-minute animated short, built in code (Remotion, this repo) and rendered to MP4. Same series and same rules as **SCROLL** (`briefs/SCROLL_brief.md`); this document lists the story and everything that is new or different.

---

## 1. The film in one sentence

A son keeps answering his mother with "Call you later" and moving a yellow **CALL MOM** sticky note to tomorrow, until he finds a voicemail he never played: *"Nothing important. I just wanted to hear your voice."* He starts one small habit: **every Sunday at 7 PM, he calls**.

**Theme:** "later" is the quiet way we spend the people we love. **Tone:** quiet, warm, honest, never preachy, no guilt-tripping and no death. **No voice acting.** Images, music and English on-screen text.

---

## 2. Style bible — what stays, what is new

Everything in SCROLL §2 applies: flat black silhouettes, dot eyes + brow strokes, the O mouth only for surprise, emotion glow (cyan = screen / coldness, amber = warmth), vignette, depth of field, over-the-shoulder blurred foregrounds, slow push-ins, fade to black between acts, white flashes at most one per second, subtitle colours (white spoken, orange excuse/temptation, light-grey italic thought), serif narration and quote cards, letter-spaced serif title card.

### Characters
- **SAM** (~30): adult build (same as Dad in SCROLL). Identifiers: a small **hair tuft** on top of the head (every shot) and a **red knitted scarf** (#d9553b, darker stripe, two short fringed tails). The scarf is old, Mom made it years ago; he wears it in every outdoor or leaving shot and takes it off indoors (office, sofa, bed). It is his identifying colour, like Mia's yellow clip.
- **MOM** (~67): about 92 % of Sam's height, slightly rounder torso. Identifiers: a **hair bun** on the back of the head and **round reading glasses** (thin white rings around the dot eyes). Always near red yarn: she is knitting him a new scarf, and **its length grows with every day he doesn't call**.
- A character's design never changes between shots.

### Worlds and colour
- **Sam's world (Acts 1–3):** cold city blue-greys (SCROLL palette #1d2129 … #5a6372); phone and laptop throw cyan (#5fd3ff).
- **Mom's world:** warm but **dim**: one lamp, muted amber (#c98f55 → #4a3626), lots of shadow. Warm, but lonely.
- **Acts 4–5:** the warmth reaches Sam: full amber lamp light, and snow outside (white) for contrast.
- Season: late autumn → deep winter (snow from mid-December). Seasons must progress correctly.

### The meter (series signature) — **LAST CALL hourglass**
1 MINUTE had a calendar, TEMPER a thermometer, SCROLL a screen-time pill. LATER has a **small hourglass card in the right third**: label **LAST CALL**, hourglass icon, readout **"3 days" → "4 days" → "10 days" → "23 days" → "47 days" → "51 days"** (Sun, Dec 20). Sand drains from the top half as days pass and the card glows colder. When he finally calls, the hourglass **flips** (sand back on top), the readout says **"today"** and it glows amber; in Act 5 it fades out.

### New subtitle kind — **phone voice**
Lines heard through a phone (voicemail, the call) are white on the dark pill with a **small speaker-wave icon** at the start of the pill, so they read as "from the phone" and not in the room. Text messages appear as **chat bubbles on the phone screen** (part of the image, not subtitles).

### Recurring props
- **Yellow sticky note "CALL MOM"** (Sam's handwriting). It moves from the fridge to the calendar to tomorrow, every day; by December a **thick stack** of them sits on the calendar.
- **Wall calendar** (Sam's kitchen): month grid, weekdays correct for 2026–27.
- **Red yarn + knitting needles + the growing scarf** (Mom).
- **Phone UIs are abstract**: generic call screen, chat bubble, voicemail list with waveform, reminder, call timer. No real app UI, logos or brand names.

## 3. Script and shot list (target 4:50–5:10, 30 fps, 1920×1080)

Day counts follow one fact: **the last call was Friday, Oct 30, 2026.**

### OPENING (0:00–0:07)
- **C0:** Black. Narration: *"Later" always sounds like a promise.*

### ACT 1: The promise
- **S1a (wide):** Mon, Nov 2, morning. Sam's small city flat, cold. Sam walks in wearing his scarf; his phone buzzes on the counter. He picks it up and frowns. LAST CALL appears: **3 days**.
- **S1b (insert):** the phone: **incoming call — Mom**. His thumb hits decline and types. Chat bubble on the phone: **"Busy. Call you later."** (also shown as an orange subtitle).
- **S2 (insert):** His hand writes **CALL MOM** on a yellow sticky note, stroke by stroke, and slaps it on the fridge.
- **S3 (medium):** Mom's room, dim warm. Mom in her armchair by the window, knitting; the red scarf is a few rows long. Her phone lights up with his message. She reads it through her glasses, smiles a little and types back: **"Okay ♥"**. Back to knitting.
- **S4 (wide):** Night. Sam comes home exhausted, opens the fridge, sees the note. Wall clock **23:40**.
  - Sam (thought): *"Too late to call now."* … *"Tomorrow."*
  - He peels the note off and sticks it on tomorrow's square of the calendar. LAST CALL **4 days**.
- **S5 (insert):** The note sits on **TUE 3**.

### ACT 2: The pattern — four beats, each opening on the calendar
- **Mon, Nov 9 — office:** a meeting, laptop glow. His phone shows **MOM** calling; he flips it face-down.
  - Sam (orange): *"Not now."* LAST CALL **10 days**.
- **Same evening — Mom:** she lifts her phone, starts to dial, hesitates, puts it down. She doesn't want to bother him. The scarf now reaches her knees. Hold, no text.
- **Sun, Nov 22 — sofa:** Sam watches something on TV (cyan glow), sees the note on the calendar.
  - Sam (orange): *"She'd call if it was important."* LAST CALL **23 days**.
- **Wed, Dec 16 — the stack:** first snow outside. On the calendar, today's square holds a **thick stack of CALL MOM notes**. He doesn't even look. This silence is the strongest beat of the act: **hold 3 s**. LAST CALL **47 days**, glowing cold.
- **Insert:** Mom's knitting basket: the scarf has grown into loops piled on the floor.

### ACT 3: The mirror
- **S6 (wide):** That night, in bed, phone-lit. He scrolls his voicemail: **MOM — 3 weeks ago** with an unplayed dot.
- **S7 (insert):** He presses play; a waveform moves.
  - Mom (phone voice): **"Hi, sweetheart. Nothing important."**
  - (beat) Mom (phone voice): **"I just wanted to hear your voice."**
  - The music drops almost to silence.
- **S8 (close):** His eyes widen, then his brows fall.
  - Sam (thought): *"Forty-seven days."*
- **S9 (flashbacks):** 3 white-flash cuts, 1.2 s apart, desaturated:
  - Mom winding the red scarf around little Sam's neck at a snowy school gate;
  - Mom waving on a station platform the day he left for the city;
  - Mom alone in the armchair, the phone silent.
- **S10 (insert):** On a fresh sticky note he does the math with a pen: **"Mom: 67" · "1 visit a year" · "= ?"**. He writes **20** and circles it.
  - Sam (thought): *"Maybe twenty more visits."*
- **QUOTE CARD 1 (8 s):** *"Procrastination is the thief of time."* — EDWARD YOUNG

### ACT 4: One small step
- **S11 (night, medium):** Sam sets a weekly reminder: **SUNDAY 7 PM — CALL MOM · Repeat: every week**. Then he books a train ticket: **City → Home · Sat, Jan 9**.
- **S12 (Sun, Dec 20, 19:00):** Dinner with friends, a warm restaurant. The reminder pops.
  - LAST CALL reads **51 days**.
  - Temptation (orange): *"Call her tomorrow."* … *"She'll understand."*
  - He stands up, winds his scarf and steps out into the snow.
- **S13 (split screen — the call):** left half the cold snowy street, right half Mom's dim room. Her phone rings; she nearly drops a needle and answers.
  - Mom (phone voice): **"Hello?"**
  - Sam (white): **"Hi, Mom. It's me."**
  - Mom's eyes become happy arcs. Mom: **"Sam!"**
  - Sam: **"How are you?"** Mom: **"Better now."**
  - The divider fades; the warm light flows across into his half and the snowflakes on his side turn amber. LAST CALL **flips → "today"**, amber.
- **S14 (time-passing montage, ghosting dissolves):** Sundays **Dec 20 → Dec 27 → Jan 3**:
  - Dec 20: his call timer **"Mom ♥ 04:12"**;
  - Dec 27: Mom sticks her own note on the wall by the family photos: **"SUN 7PM — SAM ♥"**;
  - Jan 3: the call timer reads **"52:03"**.
- **QUOTE CARD 2 (8 s):** *"The days are long, but the years are short."* — GRETCHEN RUBIN

### ACT 5: The payoff and bookend
- **S15 (train):** Sat, Jan 9. Sam at a train window, snowy fields passing, a small gift box on his knees. LAST CALL card fades out for good.
- **S16 (exterior):** Mom's small house in the snow, one warm window. He walks up the path and knocks.
- **S17 (doorway):** Mom opens: wide eyes + O mouth, then happy arcs.
  - Sam: **"Hi, Mom."** Mom: **"You came!"** They hug.
- **S18 (inside, warm):** Mom brings out the scarf she knitted while she waited. It is absurdly long. She winds it around his neck, then around her own, and the rest coils across the floor; both laugh (happy arcs). Hold 2.5 s.
- **S19 (final wide):** Pull back from her window through the snowy garden; a lit train passes in the distance; her window glows amber. Hold 4 s.
- **C-END (black):** Narration: *No calendar has a day called "Later."*
- **TITLE CARD:** **L A T E R**, tagline: *Call her today.* Hold 5 s, fade out with the music.

## 4. Sound (same rules as SCROLL)
- **No voice-over.** Placeholder score + synthesised SFX from `scripts/make_audio.py` until a real track is provided.
- **Music moods:**
  - Act 1–2: sparse, hollow piano, ticking clock pulse.
  - Act 3: near silence under the voicemail, then one soft line.
  - Act 4: warm strings enter on the call.
  - Act 5: full warm theme, gentle resolve.
- **SFX:**
  - Sam's flat: phone buzz, decline tone, keyboard taps, marker squeak, sticky-note slap, fridge door, clock tick.
  - Mom's room: knitting needle clicks (her motif), ring tone.
  - Voicemail: voicemail beep.
  - Act 4–5: restaurant murmur, snow footsteps, train rhythm, door knock.
- **Loudness:** −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1.5 dBTP, LRA ≤ 10 LU, no jump over 6 dB between neighbouring scenes; per-scene report.

## 5. Technical
- Same Remotion project as SCROLL (shared `src/characters`, `src/ui`, `src/props`), LATER code in `src/later/`, its own `timeline` and compositions prefixed `later-`.
- StickFigure gains: hair `tuft` / `bun`, `glasses`, `scarf`, and an `elder` build for Mom.
- Render 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 CRF 18, AAC 192 kbps; vertical re-framed cut as for SCROLL.

## 6. Must NOT appear
Real brands, app logos, real UIs, real people; copyrighted characters, songs or artwork; text under 32 px at 1080p; low-contrast grey text without a pill or shadow; anything suggesting Mom is ill or dying.

## 7. QA checklist
Same as SCROLL §7, plus:
- the LAST CALL counter matches the calendar dates (3 → 4 → 10 → 23 → 47);
- the scarf length only ever grows;
- calendar weekdays are correct (Nov 2 2026 = Mon … Jan 9 2027 = Sat);
- Sam's tuft in every shot (his scarf in every outdoor/leaving shot); Mom's bun and glasses in every shot.

## 8. Phases (stop and show after 1 and 2)
1. **Style frames:**
   - S1 (decline + "Busy. Call you later.");
   - S3 (Mom knitting, reading the message);
   - Act 2 stack (calendar, 47 days);
   - S7 (voicemail);
   - S13 (split-screen call);
   - S18 (the long scarf).
2. **Animatic** (10 fps, 960×540) with full timing, subtitles and cards.
3. **Final animation and render.**
4. **Audio mix + loudness pass, QA report.**

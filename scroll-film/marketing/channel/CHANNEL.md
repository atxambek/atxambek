## Name and handle
- Channel name: **Little Choices**
- Handle, in order of preference: `@littlechoices`, `@littlechoicesfilms`, `@littlechoices.films`. Use the same name on TikTok and Instagram if it is free.

## Channel description (Customisation → Basic info → Description)
```
Little Choices makes short animated films about the small, everyday choices that quietly shape a life: one more minute of a habit, one moment of anger, one evening with the phone in a box.

Silhouette characters, no voice-over, a few honest words on screen. Each film is under five minutes and made to be watched with the people you love.

Films so far:
• 1 MINUTE — about habits
• TEMPER — about anger
• SCROLL — about phones, parents and five more minutes

New film every [month / two weeks]. Subscribe and turn one small choice into a better day.
```
